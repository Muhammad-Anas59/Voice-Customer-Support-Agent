VERVE ATHLETICS — WEBSITE FAQ PAGE

Last Updated: August 2025
(Note: This is the live copy currently published on the "Help" page of the website)

Q: How long do I have to return an item?
A: You have 30 days from the date you receive your order to return it for a refund. Items must be unworn with tags attached.

Q: Do you offer free shipping?
A: Yes! Orders over $75 ship free within the USA using our Standard Shipping option (5-7 business days).

Q: Can I exchange an item for a different size?
A: Absolutely — exchanges are free within our return window as long as the item is in stock.

Q: What if my order arrives damaged?
A: Contact our support team within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the damage, and we'll send a free replacement or issue a full refund.

Q: Do you ship internationally?
A: Yes, we currently ship to Canada, the UK, the EU, and Australia.

Q: How long does a refund take once you receive my return?
A: Refunds are typically processed within 5-7 business days of us receiving the item back.

Q: Are sale items returnable?
A: Items purchased during a sale (30% off or more) are eligible for store credit only, unless the item is defective.

Q: Can I cancel my order after I place it?
A: Yes, you can cancel your order within 24 hours of placing it, as long as it hasn't shipped yet.
