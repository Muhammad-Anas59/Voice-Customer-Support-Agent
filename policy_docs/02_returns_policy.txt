VERVE ATHLETICS — RETURNS & EXCHANGES POLICY
Document Owner: Customer Experience Team
Last Updated: January 2026

1. Return Window
Items may be returned within 30 days of the delivery date for a full refund or exchange, provided they meet the conditions below.

2. Condition Requirements
- Items must be unworn, unwashed, and undamaged
- Original tags must still be attached
- Footwear must be returned in the original shoebox, which must be placed inside a shipping box (not used as the shipping box itself)
- Swimwear and underwear are final sale and cannot be returned for hygiene reasons

3. How to Initiate a Return
Customers can start a return through the "My Orders" page on our website or by emailing support with their order number. A prepaid return label is provided for domestic orders. International returns are the customer's responsibility to ship back.

4. Refund Processing Time
Once we receive the returned item, refunds are processed within 5-7 business days back to the original payment method. Store credit refunds are processed within 1-2 business days.

5. Exchanges
Exchanges for a different size or color are free of charge (no shipping cost) within the 30-day window, subject to availability. If the requested item is out of stock, we issue a refund instead.

6. Sale Items
Items purchased during a sale (marked 30% off or more) are eligible for store credit only, not a cash refund, unless the item is defective.

7. Return Shipping Costs
Returns due to customer preference (wrong size, changed mind) are free with our prepaid label.

8. Wrong Item or Our Error
If you received the wrong item, or the mistake was on our end, there is no return shipping cost. You may also request expedited replacement shipping at no charge.
