VERVE ATHLETICS — ACCOUNT, PAYMENT & LOYALTY POLICY
Document Owner: Customer Experience Team
Last Updated: May 2026

1. Accepted Payment Methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, Apple Pay, and Google Pay. We do not accept cash on delivery or bank transfers.

2. Payment Failures
If a payment fails after an order is placed, the order is held for 24 hours for the customer to retry payment before it is automatically cancelled.

3. Promo Codes
Only one promo code can be applied per order. Promo codes cannot be combined with the loyalty rewards discount. Promo codes cannot be applied retroactively to already-placed orders.

4. Verve Rewards (Loyalty Program)
Customers earn 1 point per $1 spent. 100 points = $5 off a future order. Points expire 12 months after they are earned. Points are credited 7 days after delivery (post return-window buffer) to prevent point fraud from returned items.

5. Account Deletion
Customers may request account deletion via support. Order history is retained for 7 years for tax/legal record-keeping even after account deletion, per financial regulations, but is no longer accessible to the customer.

6. Gift Cards
Gift cards do not expire and can be used for partial payment, combined with a promo code, but not combined with loyalty points redemption in the same order.
