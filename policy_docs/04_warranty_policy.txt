VERVE ATHLETICS — WARRANTY & DEFECTIVE ITEMS POLICY
Document Owner: Quality Assurance Team
Last Updated: February 2026

1. Manufacturer Warranty
All footwear comes with a 6-month warranty against manufacturing defects (sole separation, stitching failure, zipper malfunction). Apparel comes with a 3-month warranty against fabric defects (seam tearing, print peeling).

2. What Is NOT Covered
- Normal wear and tear
- Damage from improper washing (see care label)
- Damage from misuse (e.g., wearing running shoes for hiking on rocky terrain)

3. Reporting a Defect
Contact support within the warranty period with your order number and photos of the defect. Our QA team reviews within 2 business days.

4. Resolution
Approved warranty claims receive a free replacement of the same item. If out of stock, customer may choose store credit or a refund.

5. Damaged on Arrival
If an item arrives damaged during shipping (not a manufacturing defect), report it within 7 days of delivery with photos. This is handled as an immediate free replacement or refund, not a warranty claim.
