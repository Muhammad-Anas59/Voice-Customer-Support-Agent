VERVE ATHLETICS — SHIPPING POLICY
Document Owner: Logistics Team
Last Updated: March 2026

1. Processing Time
All orders are processed within 1-2 business days (Mon-Fri, excluding public holidays). Orders placed after 3 PM EST are processed the next business day.

2. Shipping Methods & Timelines (Domestic - USA)
- Standard Shipping: 5-7 business days — $5.99 (FREE on orders over $75)
- Express Shipping: 2-3 business days — $14.99
- Overnight Shipping: 1 business day — $29.99 (order must be placed before 12 PM EST)

3. International Shipping
We ship to Canada, UK, EU, and Australia.
- Standard International: 10-15 business days — $19.99
- Express International: 5-7 business days — $39.99
Customs duties and import taxes are the responsibility of the customer and are not included in the shipping cost.

4. Order Tracking
A tracking number is emailed automatically once the order ships. Tracking may take up to 24 hours to activate in the carrier's system.

5. Lost or Delayed Packages
If a package shows no tracking movement for 7+ business days (domestic) or 15+ business days (international), contact support for an investigation with the carrier. We do not issue refunds for delayed packages until the carrier confirms the package as lost (typically after 21 days domestic, 30 days international).

6. Address Errors
Verve Athletics is not responsible for packages delayed or lost due to an incorrect shipping address entered by the customer at checkout. If caught before the order ships, contact support immediately to correct it.
