VERVE ATHLETICS — ORDER CANCELLATION & MODIFICATION POLICY
Document Owner: Operations Team
Last Updated: April 2026

1. Cancellation Window
Orders can be cancelled free of charge within 1 hour of placing the order, as long as it has not yet entered the fulfillment process. After 1 hour, the order typically cannot be cancelled since fulfillment begins automatically.

2. Modifying an Order
Changes to size, color, or shipping address on an order that has NOT yet shipped can only be requested within the same 1-hour window as cancellations. This does not apply to exchanges on items you have already received — see our Returns & Exchanges policy for that. Contact support immediately — we cannot guarantee changes once fulfillment has started.

3. Cancelling After Fulfillment Has Started
If the order has already entered fulfillment, it cannot be cancelled. Customers must wait for delivery and initiate a standard return instead.

4. Order Not Yet Shipped After 5 Days
If an order has not shipped within 5 business days of being placed (excluding pre-order items), customers may request cancellation and a full refund, no questions asked.

5. Pre-Orders
Pre-order items can be cancelled any time before the item ships, since pre-orders have longer, published fulfillment windows.
